## Header logo

- Cauză stretch: `width`/`height` inventate (ex. 168×36) vs PNG real **1024×146** (~7:1).
- Fix: `LOGO_WIDTH=1024`, `LOGO_HEIGHT=146` pe `Image`.
- CSS `.saas-logo`: `width: auto`, `height` fix (1.75rem → 2rem sm+), `max-width` responsive, `object-fit: contain`, `object-position: left center`.
- `sizes`: `(max-width: 640px) 176px, 224px`.

## Footer logo

- Aceleași dimensiuni intrinsice.
- CSS `.saas-logo-footer`: height 1.5rem, `max-width` ~10.5rem, `object-fit: contain`, left-aligned.
